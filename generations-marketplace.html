Portfolio — Generations Marketplace
Overview

Rooted in tradition, built for today.

Generations Marketplace & Learning Centre is a 5th generation sustainable family farm and homesteading education centre in Ontario — a place where local food, community gathering, and hands-on learning intersect. When they brought Alora Labs on in August 2024, the heart of the brand was already there. Our job was to give it a presence that matched. We came on as their full creative partner across social media strategy and management, photography, video, graphic design, email newsletters, seasonal campaigns, and website management — building a content world that felt as warm and authentic as the farm itself.

The strategy led with storytelling — farm life as it actually is, the people behind the land, the rhythm of the seasons. Through consistent, emotionally-driven content we grew awareness around their educational programs, CSA subscriptions, and community events, and helped position Generations as a true destination. The results spoke clearly: the annual Christmas Open House, driven by a full campaign strategy, saw such strong turnout its first year that it expanded the following year into a longer, larger community marketplace event — a testament to what the right brand presence can build.
