Portfolio — Kimberly Capone Interior Design
Overview

Building a brand as refined as the spaces she creates.

Kimberly Capone Interior Design came to Alora Labs seeking a creative presence that could match the elevated, bespoke nature of her interiors work — and hold its own in an increasingly visual, competitive market. We built that presence from the ground up.

Over six years, we developed a cohesive visual language across every touchpoint: brand identity, architectural photography, social media strategy and management, video production, newsletter design, print collateral, and a full website redesign. The results were transformative — Instagram grew from 2K to 85K followers, TikTok launched from zero to 30K, and YouTube scaled from 100 to 50K subscribers. The brand's growing profile opened doors to national recognition: Kimberly was featured on CP24 and went on to serve as the featured interior designer across multiple seasons of a Mike Holmes production — a testament to the calibre of presence we helped build.
