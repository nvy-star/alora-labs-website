Overview

Precision design for medical communications.

H3C Healthcare Communications is a medical marketing agency developing education programs for physicians and healthcare professionals. Alora Labs serves as their ongoing design partner on a project basis, creating presentation decks, visual assets, and branded materials for programs targeting specialists across neurology, oncology, and other medical fields. The work requires a unique combination of creative execution and clinical precision, translating complex medical information into visually compelling, professionally polished materials.

Every project operates under tight timelines and exacting standards. From concept development through final delivery, the work must balance brand consistency, regulatory compliance, and visual impact, ensuring that medical professionals receive materials worthy of the content they're delivering. It's design work where clarity, accuracy, and professionalism aren't negotiable.
