FAQ

Common questions

What types of projects do you take on?

We work with ambitious brands across industries — from startups launching new ventures to established businesses looking to elevate their presence. Our sweet spot is comprehensive brand and marketing projects where we can apply our full range of expertise.

What's your project timeline?

Timelines vary based on scope. Brand identity projects typically take 4-8 weeks, while ongoing marketing retainers are month-to-month. We'll provide a detailed timeline during our initial consultation.

Do you work with clients outside Ontario?

Absolutely. While we're based in the Greater Toronto Area, we work with clients across Canada and internationally. Most of our collaboration happens remotely, with in-person sessions available for local clients.

What's your pricing structure?

We offer both project-based and retainer pricing depending on your needs. After our initial consultation, we'll provide a detailed proposal outlining scope, timeline, and investment. We believe in transparent pricing with no hidden fees.

Can you help with an existing brand refresh?

Yes! We love helping established brands evolve and refine their presence. Whether you need a subtle refresh or a complete transformation, we'll develop a strategy that honors your heritage while positioning you for future growth.

What makes Alora Labs different?

We bring senior-level expertise with a boutique approach. You're working directly with an experienced creative director and marketer, not a junior team. We pair strategic thinking with artistic vision, and we're personally invested in your success.
